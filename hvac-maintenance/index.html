Why Professional HVAC Maintenance Matters

Homeowners can take care of a short list: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and checking the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded look perfectly normal. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks the same as a new one. How to Prevent HVAC Maintenance Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
  • Trim plants back from the outdoor unit and give the coil a gentle rinse every spring
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
  • Change the filter every one to three months; it is the single highest-value habit there is
