Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Novato HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Novato

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly, anywhere in Novato and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and each response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Novato HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
