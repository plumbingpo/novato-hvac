Mini splits exist for the rooms central air forgets. The garage workshop, the sunroom that turns into an oven by midafternoon, the addition the ducts never reached, the Novato house that was built without ductwork at all: each of these can be heated and cooled on its own by one or more indoor heads. A slim line set carries refrigerant, power, and condensate drainage through a single three-inch wall penetration to the outdoor unit, so there is no duct system to leak. The inverter compressor speeds up and slows down with the load rather than cycling hard, which keeps the room steady, quiet, and cheap to run. In winter, the same equipment works as a cold-climate heat pump, not an afterthought.

Design is where mini split projects succeed or fail, and Novato HVAC does it deliberately. Each zone gets sized to its actual load, because an oversized head short-cycles, dehumidifies poorly, and hunts. Multi-zone systems bring their own engineering: the outdoor unit's capacity has to be matched carefully across the connected heads, line set lengths and elevation changes must stay inside the manufacturer's tables, and head placement and style affects both comfort and how the room looks. Our experienced technicians walk the space with you, place heads where the physics and the sightlines agree, and route line sets cleanly, hidden where they can be and tidy where they cannot.

Installation gets the same rigor as our largest systems. The line set is pressure-tested with nitrogen and evacuated to a micron-gauge-verified vacuum, and flare connections are torqued and leak-checked, since a weeping flare is the classic mini split failure. Condensate is routed to drain by gravity or a reliable pump, and the charge is adjusted for line set length per the manufacturer's tables. We commission every zone in both heating and cooling, then hand you the remotes with the settings explained in plain terms. Why Hire a Professional for Ductless Mini Split Installation?

Most failed mini splits we see in Novato trace back to installs that treated the job as simple. The flares are where it usually goes wrong: a joint that was poorly formed or never torqued to spec leaks slowly, and the homeowner finds out a season later when the head blows warm. Moisture is the next issue, because an install that skips the nitrogen pressure test and a micron-verified vacuum leaves water vapor inside a system whose inverter compressor is sensitive to it. Overlong or overly tall line set runs steal capacity quietly, and a drain line without enough fall will drip down the wall as soon as humidity climbs.

What separates good ductless work from bad is discipline: sizing. Even the smallest indoor heads are oversized for plenty of bedrooms, and an oversized head cycles constantly, leaves moisture in the air, and never finds a steady state. Novato HVAC sizes zones honestly and installs them with the same care, torquing and testing every flare, proving the line set with nitrogen, pulling a measured vacuum, adjusting charge for the run, and testing each head in both modes. The refrigerant side is federally regulated and the electrical side calls for a properly sized circuit and disconnect, so neither is a place to improvise. What you get is a system that stays silent and efficient for fifteen years instead of one that mysteriously underperforms.

How to Prevent Ductless Mini Split Installation Problems

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Schedule professional cleaning of the indoor coil and blower wheel before buildup turns into growth
  • Book annual professional service to verify charge, flares, and drainage while fixes are cheap
  • Resist turning the system off and on; an inverter is most efficient holding one steady temperature
  • Glance at the condensate drain outlet in summer to confirm it is actually dripping
  • Rinse or wash the indoor unit's filter screens monthly; they load up faster than furnace filters
  • Give the outdoor unit open space in every season, free of drifts, debris, and mowing clippings
  • In winter, expect defrost pauses and light steam from the outdoor unit; solid ice means call us
